# What the Heihe block is taken from

This pass only dresses the blocks already in the slice: 中央街 down to the Heilongjiang promenade, and the ice in front of that bank. The camera is still the fixed isometric view. Nothing here is a photograph pasted onto a mesh. The notes are what the materials were matched to.

## What was drawn, not copied

- Every material is a shader. No photo is used as a texture. Tile, snow, ice and window normals are computed in the shader.
- People are original meshes: a padded coat, hood, scarf and boots for civilians; a winter suit, helmet, vest and rifle for the infantry. The kit is the generic shape of current Russian winter infantry (helmet, vest, rifle, mottled winter suit), not a named unit and not a copied uniform plate.
- The far bank is a row of plain lit blocks on the Russian side of the imported river, about 750 m north of this promenade. This camera sits northeast of the player and looks southwest, back at the Chinese street, so those blocks are behind the lens. The river shot shows the ice, the rail and the Chinese bank. It does not pretend the Russian skyline fits in a 40 m isometric frame.
- Explosions, muzzle flashes and tracers are game effects. The whistle and the drone buzz are still the synthesised tones from `tools/audio/gen_audio.py`.
- Snow depth, which shops have a roller shutter down, and which windows have curtains are chosen, not surveyed.
