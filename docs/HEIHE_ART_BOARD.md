# Art board for the 中央街 block

The camera is a close isometric so one block fills the frame and roofs show. Figures stay low-poly. The aim is that the street reads as a Northeast Chinese winter street, not as a generic tiled grid.

## How that became the block

Along the shop edge the kit changes every five bays: a short pastel Russian-style front, a six-storey brick walk-up with a roof tank, a two-storey shop row with an awning, and a beige tile block with blue glass. Roofs are flat and snowy so the close camera can see them. Outside the player's pool the street falls off into blue dusk and fog.

## Still not the photographs

The bays repeat inside each type. Arches are a sphere cap, not masonry. The 黑A plates are a yellow label on a black rectangle, not a real plate. Cars are boxes. The far bank in the river frame is still a shortened stand-in. Soldiers are white blocks with a rifle, not a photographed winter uniform. No overhead wires.
