# Russian forces as observed in the Russo-Ukrainian war (2022 to late 2026)

Research notes for the enemy data table. Compiled 2026-11-15 from open sources.

Purpose: game balance only. Everything here is about observed effects, rates, ranges, timings, organisation and failure modes. Nothing here describes how to build, modify or operate a weapon.

## How to read this file

- **Period** is the period the figure describes, not the publication date.
- **Confidence** is about how well the figure is supported, not about how important it is.
  - high: a named research institute or UN/NGO report, read directly in this session.
  - medium: one credible named source, or a figure relayed by a reliable outlet.
  - low: a single participant's claim, a party to the war talking about its enemy or itself, a manufacturer or state-media figure, or a number I could only see second-hand.
- **†** after a source means the number was taken from a search-engine summary of that page; the page itself was not opened in this session. Check before relying on it.
- **"Ukrainian-side figure"** means the number describes Ukrainian equipment or practice. It is included only where no Russian-side number exists and the equipment is the same class (for example FPV drones). Treat as a proxy.
- **Not found** means I looked and did not find a sourced number. It is not an estimate of zero.
- Almost every casualty, hit-rate and "share of losses" number in this war comes from one of the two belligerents. Where a figure is a belligerent's claim, the row says so.
- Oryx counts were read from the live page on 2026-11-15 by automated extraction of the page's type headings. Re-check any count you intend to quote.

## Things commonly believed that the sources do not support

1. **"An FPV drone is a one-shot precision kill."** The best field study puts 60 to 80% failing to reach the target, and says most hits on armour do not destroy it. A tank often takes about a dozen. (RUSI 2025; IStories 2025.)
2. **"Drones have replaced artillery."** Officers with the best drone units still asked for artillery; a mortar answers in under 5 minutes against about 15 for an FPV; guns work in weather that grounds drones. (RUSI 2025; War on the Rocks 2025.)
3. **"Drones see everything, always."** Coverage is near-total only within about 3 km of the line and only in flying weather. Fog, rain, low cloud and jamming open long gaps, and Russian infiltration is timed for them. (RUSI 2025; ISW via Euromaidan Press 2025.)
4. **"A jammer makes you safe."** About a fifth or more of Russian FPVs are fibre-optic and cannot be jammed; jammer radii are short and mostly makers' claims; jamming also brings down friendly drones. (FPRI 2026; War on the Rocks 2025.)
5. **"Russian attacks are armoured columns" or "human waves".** By 2025 tanks were scarce near the front and the standard attack was a handful of men on foot or on bikes, sent in sequence, not in mass. (RUSI 2025, both papers.)
6. **"Artillery must shoot and scoot."** In 2024 both sides fired for long periods from dug-in, netted pits spaced over 500 m apart, and moved mainly because of glide bombs. (RUSI 2025.)
7. **"Glide bombs are precision weapons."** They depend on satellite navigation, go wide when jammed, and are used in volume against fixed positions and built-up areas. No measured miss distance was found. (JAPCC 2025; RUSI 2023.)
8. **"Lancet hits 78% of the time."** That figure is a tally of videos the Russian side chose to publish. RUSI called the success rate limited; one Russian unit was reported at 3 hits in about 100. (Army Recognition 2025; RUSI 2023; United24 2026, low confidence.)
9. **"Orlan-cued artillery arrives in seconds."** The best case observed is 3 to 5 minutes, the drone does not generate coordinates itself, and without a drone it is 20 to 30 minutes. (RUSI 2023; ESD 2025.)
10. **"Every Russian soldier has night vision."** Thermal sights are not standard issue and are bought by volunteers; UK intelligence reported shortages and little night training. Night danger comes mainly from thermal drones, not from riflemen. (The Insider; UK MoD 2023.)
11. **"Winter stops the fighting" or "snow hides you."** Cold shortens battery life and ice grounds fibre drones, but snow shows footprints and cold sharpens thermal contrast, and assaults increase in fog. (Kyiv Independent 2026; Euromaidan Press 2026.)
12. **"Most Shahed-type drones get through."** Typically 75 to 90% are stopped; the damage comes from volume. (CSIS 2025; ISIS 2026.)
13. **"Occupation violence is random."** The documented pattern is systematic: door-to-door searches, phone and tattoo checks, filtration with biometric registration, and detention aimed at men, veterans, officials and activists. (HRW 2022; OHCHR 2022 and 2023.)
14. **"Drones cause 90% of all losses."** Estimates run from a quarter to nine-tenths depending on who is counting and what is counted; RUSI's 60 to 70% for equipment in 2024 is the best-supported, and RUSI attributes part of it to a shortage of shells. (RUSI 2025; Kyiv Post.)
15. **"We know how these weapons behave at minus 25 °C."** We do not. The coldest conditions in this war were about minus 20 °C for a short spell in early 2026. Anything colder is extrapolation.
